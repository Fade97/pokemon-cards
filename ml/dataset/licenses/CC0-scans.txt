User scans in this dataset (the files in scans/ and the rows of scans.csv)

To the extent possible under law, the contributors have waived all copyright and related or neighbouring rights to
their scans by dedicating them to the public domain under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. Each scan's `consent`
column names the consent text its contributor accepted in the app.

Legal code: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The cards shown in the scans are the artwork of The Pokemon Company, Nintendo, Creatures and GAME FREAK. CC0 covers
the contributors' photographs, not the card designs, trademarks or artwork.
